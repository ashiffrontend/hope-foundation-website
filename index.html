About Hope Foundation

Built on Uncompromising Transparency & Compassion

Founded in 2014, Hope Foundation is a legally recognized, audited, non-profit public charitable trust dedicated to serving impoverished families regardless of caste, creed, religion, or background.

12+ Years of Dedicated Uninterrupted Humanitarian Service

Our Mission, Vision & Purpose

We believe that no child should go to bed hungry, no sick patient should be denied life-saving treatment due to lack of funds, and no victim of natural disasters should feel forgotten. We combine grassroots emergency logistics with long-term rehabilitation through schooling and vocational skills.

Compassion First
Serving with dignity, warmth, and respect for every beneficiary.
100% Integrity
Zero tolerance for corruption or waste. Every rupee is accounted for.
Radical Transparency
Audited annual financial reports, 80G tax certificates, and public ledgers.
Inclusive Community
Empowering women, orphans, elderly, disabled individuals, and rural youth.

"Charity is not merely about pity or giving away what we do not need. It is about restoring human dignity, standing shoulder-to-shoulder with our vulnerable brothers and sisters in their darkest hour, and empowering them so they never have to beg for a meal again."

Dr. Ashifa Parveen Founder & Managing Trustee, Hope Foundation | Ashif Web Studio Humanitarian Initiative

Our 12-Year Journey of Impact

From a small weekly community kitchen in 2014 to a nationwide humanitarian relief network

2014
The First Kitchen
Started with 50 hot meals distributed every Sunday to homeless families in Delhi and Lucknow railway stations.
2017
Official Trust & 80G Registration
Registered under the Indian Trusts Act with 12A and 80G tax exemption, enabling corporate CSR and nationwide donor trust.
2020
COVID-19 Pandemic Shield
Supplied 1.8 Million dry ration kits, 45,000 oxygen cylinder refills, and launched 24/7 tele-medicine helplines during lockdowns.
2023
Medical Wings & Digital Schools
Inaugurated 12 mobile medical dispensaries and 35 digital computer training labs for slum youth and rural girls.
2026
Nationwide Relief Network
Expanded across 15+ states with 800+ completed medical camps, 20,000+ families lifted from extreme poverty, and zero compromise on accountability.

Real Lives Transformed

Stories of Courage, Dignity & Hope

Behind every statistic is a human soul whose life was altered because someone chose not to look away. Read their genuine journey in their own words.

"When my husband passed away in the floods, I had no income and three young daughters to feed. Neighbors told me to pull them out of school. Hope Foundation enrolled me into their 6-month tailoring program and gifted me an electric sewing machine. Today I earn ₹16,000 every month stitching uniforms, and my oldest daughter is preparing for her 10th board exams!"

Farzana Begum Widow & Vocational Graduate • Dhubri, Assam

"I used to collect plastic scrap at the railway platform. An uncle from Hope Foundation came with notebooks, pencils, and sandwiches. He didn't just teach us letters—he believed in us. I passed their basic digital course last year and now work as a data operator in an IT logistics hub in Noida. My mother no longer works as a domestic maid."

Mohammad Rizwan (Age 19) Former Street Child, Now Certified Tech Specialist

"I had bilateral cataracts for 4 years. Everything was a grey haze; I could not even see my grandchildren's faces or walk without falling. The government hospital had a 1-year waiting list. Hope Foundation brought their bus, tested my eyes, performed laser surgery, gave me free medicines, and sent me home with vision restored. May God bless every donor."

Ramrati Devi (Age 68) Cataract Surgery Beneficiary • Muzaffarnagar

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Find immediate answers about 80G tax exemptions, donation procedures, volunteer guidelines, and financial transparency.

Yes, absolutely! Hope Foundation is an approved charitable trust under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act. Indian taxpayers can claim a 50% deduction on their taxable income for donations made to our programs. We immediately generate an official 80G tax receipt along with Form 10BE filing reference.
Instantly! As soon as you complete your donation on our portal, a formatted 80G digital receipt with official trust seal, registration number, and PAN record is generated right on your screen and emailed directly to your inbox.
We practice radical transparency. Every project publishes photo documentation, GPS coordinates of distribution camps, and audited annual balance sheets certified by independent Chartered Accountants. You can also visit any of our community kitchens and medical camps unannounced at any time.
Anyone aged 16 or above with a compassionate heart and clean record is welcome to apply! We have roles for college students, doctors, working corporate professionals, teachers, and senior citizens. You can choose weekend-only, evening, or emergency disaster response commitments.
Yes. Hope Foundation is fully registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs with Unique CSR Registration Number CSR00018942 and eligible to receive CSR funds under Schedule VII of the Companies Act 2013 for hunger relief, healthcare, education, and rural water projects.
